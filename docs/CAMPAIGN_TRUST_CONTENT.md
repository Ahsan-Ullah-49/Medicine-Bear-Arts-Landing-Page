# Campaign trust content and official destinations

Last reviewed: 2026-11-05.

The campaign remains product-first. The trust content appears after the campaign product area, and official-store links appear as a restrained footer resource list. The normal Medicine Bear Arts mega-navigation is not duplicated here.

## Verified sources used

- Artist and brand summary: `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/artist-bio` and `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/about-us`
- Shipping, returns, exchanges, materials, and contact-response guidance: `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/faqs`
- Secondary browse destination: `https://medicinebeararts.com/collections/all`
- Campaign contact destination: the client-supplied WhatsApp number configured in `js/products.js`

## Content choices

- The short bio identifies Jadeon Rathgeber (Medicine Bear) as a Cree artist, storyteller, and advocate from Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation.
- Shipping does not state a flat price or delivery time because the official site does not publish one in the FAQ. It directs shoppers to the secure checkout for available options and charges.
- Returns/exchanges reflect the official FAQ: a full refund for an undamaged return within 30 days, with equal-value exchange guidance for an unused item after that period.
- Authenticity copy is deliberately factual: the official FAQ identifies .925 silver, brass, and gold options; nickel-free metals; and purity hallmarks.
