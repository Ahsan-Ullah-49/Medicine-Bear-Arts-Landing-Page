# Campaign trust content and destinations

Last reviewed: 2026-11-05.

The campaign remains product-first. A dedicated Meet the Artist / story section appears after the product area, followed by a separate purchase-confidence FAQ section. The footer provides concise columns of secondary routes without duplicating the normal Medicine Bear Arts mega-navigation.

## Verified sources used

- Artist and brand summary: `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/artist-bio` and `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/about-us`
- Shipping, returns, exchanges, and contact-response guidance: `https://medicinebeararts.com/pages/faqs`
- Secondary browse destination: `https://medicinebeararts.com/collections/all`
- Campaign contact destination: the client-supplied WhatsApp number configured in `js/products.js`

## Content choices

- The short bio identifies Jadeon Rathgeber (Medicine Bear) as a Cree artist, storyteller, and advocate from Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation. It links to the full artist bio and brand story rather than reproducing either page.
- The FAQ contains only the customer-confidence topics requested by the owner: the artist, Indigenous artist ownership, the Medicine Bear Arts story, worldwide shipping, shipping charges, returns/exchanges, and contact options.
- Shipping does not state a flat price or delivery time because the source does not publish one. It directs shoppers to checkout for destination-specific delivery options and charges.
- Returns/exchanges reflect the source: a full refund for an undamaged return within 30 days, equal-value exchange guidance for an unused piece after that period, and repair or replacement for a manufacturing defect.
- The story layout uses the client-provided Jadeon portrait and Medicine Bear Arts logo. No generated or unapproved portrait is used.

## Current campaign catalog

The campaign now contains 12 client-approved products. The layout and product configuration remain extensible if the client approves future additions; do not select further pieces automatically from the full store.
