The artist behind the work

Meet Jadeon Rathgeber.

Maskihkîy-Muskwa (Medicine Bear) · Cree Artist · Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation

Jadeon Rathgeber, known as Maskihkîy-Muskwa (Medicine Bear), is a Cree artist, storyteller and advocate from Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation.

In 2006, Jadeon began a healing journey through ceremony, mentorship and carving. Through Medicine Bear Arts, he continues to offer fine jewellery and heirloom pieces crafted with traditional knowledge.

Who is the artist behind Medicine Bear Arts?+

Medicine Bear Arts is the studio of Jadeon Rathgeber, known as Maskihkîy-Muskwa (Medicine Bear): a Cree artist, storyteller and advocate from Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation.

Is Medicine Bear Arts Indigenous artist-owned?+

Yes. The work is created and shared through Jadeon’s studio. His family roots and artistic practice are grounded in Peter Ballantyne Cree Nation and the traditions, artistry and stories of Turtle Island.

What is the story behind Medicine Bear Arts?+

Since 2006, Jadeon’s practice has grown from carving, ceremony and mentorship. Each piece is part of a wider commitment to storytelling, cultural continuity and connection through art.

Where do you ship?+

Medicine Bear Arts can ship anywhere in the world. If the delivery option you need is not available at checkout, please get in touch and the team will help.

How much is shipping?+

What if I need a return or exchange?+

An undamaged piece returned within 30 days is eligible for a full refund. After 30 days, an unused and undamaged piece may be exchanged for another item of equal value. Manufacturing defects are repaired or replaced at no cost.
